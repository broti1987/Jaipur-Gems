The House of High Jewellery

Six generations of expertise

Jaipur Gems opened in Mumbai in 1974, but the family’s understanding of gemstones runs six generations deep. That knowledge is the inheritance, the reason a piece is proportioned the way it is, and still worth wearing decades later.

Crafted for Eternity

One atelier, start to finish

Every piece is drawn, set, and finished by our own craftsmen. We buy no pre-set jewellery and outsource nothing. Stones are chosen before a piece is drawn, slower, and the only way we can guarantee it.

What Sets Us Apart

It begins on paper

A piece is argued over in pencil long before any gold is cut. Proportion is settled here, and proportion is what you notice decades later.
