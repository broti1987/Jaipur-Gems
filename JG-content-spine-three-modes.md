# Content Spine — Main Section (Three Modes, Two Folds Each)

Swappable editorial section on the UAE homepage. One mode is live at a time, set by campaign. Each mode runs two folds: **Fold A** opens with a proposition, **Fold B** substantiates it.

Bodies are capped at 40 words. Quiet-luxury register, no superlatives without proof, no client names.

---

## MODE 1 — BRAND
*The House of Sacheti. Default mode between campaigns.*

### Fold A — The proposition

**Overline**
THE HOUSE OF SACHETI

**Headline**
Six generations of knowing stones

**Body** (38 words)
Jaipur Gems opened in Mumbai in 1974, but the family's understanding of gemstones runs six generations deep. That knowledge is the inheritance — the reason a piece is proportioned the way it is, and still worth wearing decades later.

**Caption**
The first Jaipur Gems workshop, Worli, Mumbai, 1974.

### Fold B — The substantiation

**Overline**
MADE IN OUR OWN ATELIER

**Headline**
Nothing here is assembled

**Body** (39 words)
Every piece is drawn, set, and finished by our own karigars at Starfire Gems. We buy no pre-set jewellery and outsource nothing. Stones are chosen before a piece is drawn — slower, and the only way we can guarantee it.

|---|---|---|
| Founded in Mumbai | Karigars in our atelier | Generations in gemstones |

## MODE 2 — CATEGORY
*Written for Nuvo Polki Jadau. Same structure carries to other categories when needed.*

### Fold A — What it is

**Overline**
NUVO POLKI JADAU

**Headline**
Uncut diamonds, held by gold alone

**Body** (40 words)
Jadau is the Mughal-era craft of setting stones into gold with lac — no claws, no prongs. Polki are natural diamonds left uncut, so they hold light rather than throw it. Nuvo is that craft, drawn to lighter proportions.

**Explainer panel**

- **Polki** — Natural diamonds, uncut and unpolished. Softer light, as the stone was found.
- **Jadau** — Stones seated in 22-karat gold using lac, entirely by hand.

---

### Fold B — Why it endures

**Overline**
WHY IT LASTS

**Headline**
Made to be worn, and worn again

**Body** (39 words)
A Jadau piece takes weeks in a single pair of hands. The gold is 22-karat, warmer and denser than 18. Worked by hand throughout, it is built to be worn now and passed on later.

**Detail list**

- **Set in lac** — No prongs. The gold itself does the holding.
- **22-karat gold** — Deeper colour, greater weight.

## Build notes

**Fold anatomy** (identical across modes, so one component serves all three)
Overline → Headline → Body (≤40 words) → optional block (proof row · explainer panel · detail list · detail line).

**Character guides**
Overline ≤ 32 chars · Headline ≤ 42 desktop / ≤ 28 mobile · Body 210–260 chars.

**Alternation** Fold A image-left / text-right, Fold B text-left / image-right, so the folds read as a pair.
